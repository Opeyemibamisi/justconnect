Meet our leader

Abiodun Kehinde Victor

Lead Creative Director & Founder, Just Connect

Abiodun is a freelance tutor and entrepreneur who has worked with many establishments. That experience gave him a deep understanding of the socio-economy and of how the chain of work flows, from the people who offer a service to those who need it.

He is also the Lead Creative Director of De’komrade Tutorial, which provides tutoring both online and on site through freelance tutors.

A visionary and the pioneer behind Just Connect, he is known as a valuable person who genuinely helps others. Long before Just Connect had a name, he was already connecting people to people. Today, he is turning that gift into a platform that opens doors for everyone.
